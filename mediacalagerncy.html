What we do

Home delivery

Orders are packed and sent to your address the same day when placed before evening. Free above ₹500.

Wholesale supply

Regular stock for clinics, nursing homes, other chemists and hospitals, with GST billing and credit terms on request.

Monthly medicine kits

Tell us your regular medicines once. We pack the same list every month and remind you before it runs out.

Health devices

BP monitors, glucometers, nebulisers, pulse oximeters and thermometers, checked before they leave the shop.

Surgical & hospital items

Gloves, syringes, dressings, masks and disposables in retail packs or by the carton.

Expiry-checked stock

Every strip is checked for batch and expiry date before it is billed. We do not sell medicines close to expiry without telling you.

About Shree Balaji Medical Agency

We are a medical agency supplying medicines, surgical items and health devices to families, clinics and other chemists. Our work is simple: keep the right stock, bill it honestly and deliver on time.

Our pharmacist checks every prescription order before it is packed. If a medicine is unavailable, we call you with an alternative of the same salt instead of leaving you waiting.
